1. Individual rolls are noisy

A single die roll can be any value from 1 to 6. The original distribution is not bell-shaped.

2. Averages are more stable

When several dice are rolled together, the sample mean is more likely to be near the center.

Sample mean = sum of rolls / n

3. Larger n narrows the distribution

The standard error decreases as sample size increases, so sample means vary less.

SE = σ / √n
