2. Where does the safety stock come from?

Uses the Section 1 inputs. The variance of lead-time demand has a demand part and a lead-time part.

From Section 1

Variances add, not standard deviations. The lead-time part is multiplied by d², which is why it usually dominates. Remember KFC (slides 3 and 7): a late truck costs far more safety stock than a busy day.
